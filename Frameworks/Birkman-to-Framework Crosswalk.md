| MBTI Dichotomy | Birkman Evidence | Confidence |

| **I — Introversion** | Blue Usual Behavior (reflective, selectively sociable, thoughtful); Yellow Needs (concentrate on tasks, don't interrupt); Stress pattern (withdrawn under pressure) | **Very High** |

| **J — Judging** | Yellow Needs (organized approach, consistent rules, structured); Structured thinking strength; Stress: rigid, over-insistent on rules; Low Administrative interest is about clerical work, not about preferring structure | **Medium-High** |

### Alternative: INTP (The Logician)

If Dan's J/P preference is weaker than assumed: INTP (Ti-Ne-Si-Fe) would
emphasize analytical depth (Ti) over systematic execution (Te). The Blue
Interests creative drive combined with Yellow structured needs creates genuine
tension — INTJ resolves it toward structure (J), INTP toward exploration (P).

**Settled inference**: INTJ is the better fit because Dan's Birkman Needs
(systematic, organized environment) and Strengths (structured thinking,
organized approach) strongly suggest a J preference. However, the INTP
alternative should be retained as a secondary inference.

### MBTI Cognitive Stack (INTJ)

1. **Dominant: Introverted Intuition (Ni)** — Pattern recognition, future-focus,
   "aha" insights. Maps to Blue Interests (innovation, future-orientation).
2. **Auxiliary: Extraverted Thinking (Te)** — Systematic execution, organizing
   external world. Maps to Yellow Needs (organized approach, consistent rules).
3. **Tertiary: Introverted Feeling (Fi)** — Personal values, authenticity. Maps
   to Emotional intelligence strength and one-on-one sensitivity.
4. **Inferior: Extraverted Sensing (Se)** — Present-moment awareness. Maps to
   Artistic interest and visual appeal strength.

  Type 1's moral righteousness doesn't fit Dan's reflective, creative profile.
- **Not Type 9 (Peacemaker)**: Dan's "everyone wins" collaborative strength
  suggests some Type 9 traits, but his core motivation is knowledge and
  competence, not peace.

### Type 5w6 Career Implications
- Type 5: Drawn to roles requiring deep expertise, analysis, and intellectual
  autonomy. Thrives when trusted to master complex systems.
- Wing 6: Adds security-consciousness, systematic approach, and loyalty to
  institutions/teams. Complements Type 5's tendency toward isolation with

### DISC Style: CS "Perfectionist" / "Specialist"

The CS blend describes someone who is:
- Systematic, precise, and analytical (C-primary)
- Patient, methodical, and supportive (S-secondary)
- Prefers working with FACTS and SYSTEMS over people or persuasion
- Excels at maintaining quality standards and deep expertise
- Communicates formally and factually
- Needs clear expectations and time to produce quality work

This maps directly to Dan's Blue/Yellow bridge: C provides the systematic
execution (Yellow), S provides the reflective, patient approach (Blue).

### What DISC adds beyond Birkman
Birkman tells us Dan NEEDS systematic environments. DISC tells us he'll
ACT systematically in any environment — he brings structure with him.
