# Parachute Flower Exercise — Dan Bechtel Profile

> **Method note:** This profile was NOT completed through Bolles' self-report
> exercise. It was DERIVED by mapping Dan's Birkman assessment data, Enneagram
> type, MBTI, DISC, Big Five, and Holland Code onto the seven-petal framework.
> This approach combines the objectivity of psychometric assessment with the
> practical structure of Parachute's self-inventory.

---

## Petal 1: People / Work Environment

**Ideal colleagues and work relationships:**

| Dimension | Profile | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Holland Code of coworkers** | I (Investigative) and A (Artistic) primary; compatible with R (Realistic). Avoid E (Enterprising) and S (Social) dominant environments. | Birkman → Holland Code IAR |
| **Team size** | Small teams (1-4 people) or solo work. One-on-one relationships preferred. | Blue Usual Behavior (selectively sociable); Yellow Needs (time alone or with one or two others) |
| **Communication style** | Written over verbal. Thoughtful, precise, well-documented. | Literary interest 92%; Written word strength; INTJ (written > spoken) |
| **Management style** | Autonomous, trust-based. Clear expectations, minimal oversight. "Tell me the rules, then trust me to execute." | Yellow Needs (organized approach, consistent rules, invite input) |
| **Values in colleagues** | Competence, intellectual honesty, systematic thinking, creative insight | INTJ + Type 4w5 values |
| **Avoid** | Large, noisy, socially-focused teams; high-pressure sales culture; bureaucratic/political environments | Low Social Service 21%; Low Persuasive 32%; Yellow Stress (rigid under chaos) |

**Parachute-Petal-1 bottom line:** Dan works best with small groups of
analytical, creative, systematic thinkers who value written communication
and give each other space for deep work.

**Parachute-Petal-3 bottom line:** Dan's transferable skill profile is D-T-P:
Data-first (analyzing, organizing, synthesizing), Things-second (operating,
integrating, precision), People-third and only in written/one-on-one modes.
This makes him ideally suited for technical individual contributor roles —
Systems Administrator, Security Analyst, Technical Writer — and contraindicates
people-management tracks.

### The Parachute Advantage

Bolles' framework adds a PRACTICAL layer that psychometric assessments alone
cannot provide:

1. **Informational interviewing strategy** — Dan's INTJ/Blue introvert
   preference for one-on-one structured conversations makes this an ideal
   job-search method for him.

2. **The networking mindset shift** — from "asking for a job" (stressful
   for low-Persuasive types) to "learning about a field" (natural for
   high-Investigative types).

3. **Skills articulation** — the Party Exercise gives Dan language to
   describe what he does in transferable terms, not just job titles.

4. **Conditional prioritization** — knowing which working conditions are
   non-negotiable (quiet, autonomy, systematic) prevents accepting a job
   that will trigger his Yellow stress pattern.
