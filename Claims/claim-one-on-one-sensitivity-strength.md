# anitalmid-birkman-11: One-on-one sensitivity supports consultative IT within technical constraints

**Source:** [[Dan Bechtel Birkman Profile]] — Birkman Basics Report (G6HVDQ, 2025-09-05)

## The Claim

Dan has significant interpersonal sensitivity strengths — Strength #6 ("sensitive and insightful in one-on-one relationships"), Strength #9 ("appreciate an environment where everyone wins together"), and Strength #10 ("understand and relate well to others' feelings"). These support consultative IT roles: technical advisory, solutions architecture (with stakeholder interaction), IT business analysis, and vendor technical liaison. However, low Social Service interest (21%) and low Persuasive interest (32%) mean this strength should express through TECHNICAL advisory — explaining systems to stakeholders, not managing people or selling solutions.

## Evidence

### Birkman strengths:
- #6: "sensitive and insightful in one-on-one relationships"
- #9: "appreciate an environment where everyone wins together"
- #10: "understand and relate well to others' feelings, and you are aware of the extent to which these can affect performance"

### Birkman constraints:
- Social Service: 21% — NOT drawn to helping professions
- Persuasive: 32% — NOT drawn to influencing others
- Usual Behavior ◆ in BLUE: "selectively sociable" — deep one-on-one, not broad social

### Career history:
- AppleCare iOS Chat Advisor: one-on-one problem-solving, not sales
- Technology Solutions Partner: "Recommended and deployed new technology solutions, increasing staff workflow efficiency" — consultative, not managerial

## Confidence

**Rating:** high

**Rationale:** Three explicit strength statements provide strong evidence. Career history shows the pattern in practice. The constraint from low Social Service/Persuasive is well-supported by quantitative scores.

## Stakes

This claim prevents misreading Dan's interpersonal sensitivity as "people person" or "management track." It channels the recommendation toward technical roles with advisory components (senior engineer explaining architecture, security analyst presenting findings) rather than people-management roles (IT manager, service desk supervisor, customer success).

## Edges

**Supports:**
- [[claim-systems-administrator-primary-fit]] — Senior sysadmin involves explaining technical decisions to non-technical stakeholders
- [[claim-security-analyst-fit]] — Security analysts must communicate findings to leadership

**Contradicts:**
- [[claim-persuasive-interest-low]] — The sensitivity strengths could be misinterpreted as persuasion aptitude; the Birkman data clarifies they are distinct

**Challenged by:**
- [[claim-social-service-low]] — If Social Service were higher, IT service management would be viable; at 21%, it is not

## Assessment

Interpersonal sensitivity is a valuable differentiator in technical roles — it enables the bridge between technical work and organizational impact. But it must be understood within its Birkman constraints: Dan is an empathetic technologist, not a people-manager-in-waiting.
