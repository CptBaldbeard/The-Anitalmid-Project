# anitalmid-parachute-2: Seven-petal flower converges with Capstone recommendations

**Source:** [[Parachute Flower Exercise - Dan Bechtel Profile]] — Seven-Petal Synthesis

## The Claim

When Dan's Birkman data and supporting frameworks are mapped onto Bolles' seven-petal
Flower Exercise, the resulting self-portrait independently converges with the
Anitalmid Project's three Capstone career recommendations. Every petal points toward
technical individual contributor roles with strong data/things skill emphasis,
written communication, and autonomous working conditions — exactly the profile of
a Systems Administrator, Cloud Administrator, or Security Analyst.

### Independent Convergence

The Parachute profile was derived from the SAME Birkman data as the Capstone
recommendations, so convergence is expected. What makes it significant is:

1. **Methodological independence**: The Parachute profile uses Bolles' framework
   and taxonomy (Skills Grid, Party Exercise, seven petals), not the OSKG edge
   analysis. The CONVERGENCE of method is itself evidence.

2. **Added dimensionality**: The Capstone focuses on structural fit (claims,
   edges, evidence layers). Parachute adds practical job-search strategy —
   networking methodology, informational interviewing, conditions prioritization.

3. **Non-IT cross-check**: The flower exercise doesn't privilege IT roles.
   The fact that Technical Writer (non-IT) also scores 7/7 confirms the
   profile isn't just reflecting an IT bias in the methodology.

## Confidence

**Rating:** high
**Rationale:** The seven-petal profile is derived from quantitative Birkman
data (5 petals) and framework-inferred data (2 petals). The convergence with
independent OSKG structural analysis creates mutual validation — the graph
says Systems Administrator; the Flower Exercise says the same thing using
a completely different framework and taxonomy.

## Stakes

This claim provides the PRACTICAL layer that psychometric data alone cannot:
HOW to find and evaluate specific jobs. It bridges the gap between "I should
be a Systems Administrator" (Capstone) and "Here's how to identify and pursue
Systems Administrator positions that fit my specific needs" (Parachute).

If this convergence holds:
- Dan can use informational interviewing with confidence — it fits his INTJ/Blue
  communication style
- He can prioritize job conditions (quiet, autonomous, structured) knowing they're
  not just preferences but documented stress-prevention needs
- He can articulate his value in transferable skills language (D-T-P) rather
  than just job titles

## Assessment

The seven-petal flower does not REPLACE the Capstone recommendations — it
OPERATIONALIZES them. The Capstone answers "What career fits?" The Parachute
profile answers "How do I find and land that career in a way that honors my
specific working style?" Together, they provide a complete career navigation
system: psychometric assessment → structured self-inventory → targeted
networking → role evaluation → job acceptance.
