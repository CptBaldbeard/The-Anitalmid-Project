# Claim: Birkman Interest Triad Converges on Cultural Belief Systems Research

## Statement

Dan's three highest Birkman interest scores — Scientific 92%, Literary 92%, and
Artistic 94% — converge on the core activities of cultural/anthropological research:
systematic investigation of belief systems (Scientific), written communication
of findings (Literary), and creative interpretation of symbolic and cultural
materials (Artistic). This convergence is stronger for cultural research than for
any currently profiled IT role, including Systems Administration.

## Evidence

The three interests map to the three phases of ethnographic/folkloric research:

| Birkman Interest | Research Phase | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| Scientific 92% | Investigation | Systematic cultural analysis, comparative methodology, source criticism |
| Literary 92% | Documentation | Ethnographic writing, narrative construction, scholarly publication |
| Artistic 94% | Interpretation | Symbolic analysis, meaning reconstruction, creative synthesis |

Additional Birkman strengths supporting this mapping:
- **Investigating #1** — the cardinal strength of a researcher
- **Written word** — the primary output medium
- **Structured thinking** — systematic comparative methodology
- **Ambiguity handling** — essential for interpreting fragmentary cultural evidence

## Confidence: MEDIUM

Framework-inference only. Birkman measures interests, not demonstrated ability in
these domains. No direct experience in cultural/anthropological research exists to
validate this convergence.

## Implication

The Birkman profile's STRONGEST signals point toward work that Dan has never
professionally done. This is not a contradiction — it documents unexpressed
aptitude. The IT path is experience-validated; this path is aptitude-validated.
