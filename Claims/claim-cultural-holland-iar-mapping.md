# Claim: Holland Code IAR Maps to Cultural Anthropology and Folklore Careers

## Statement

Dan's IAR Holland Code (Investigative-Artistic-Realistic) maps structurally to
careers in cultural anthropology, folklore studies, comparative religion, and
museum curation. The O*NET-assigned RIASEC codes for these occupations (IRA for
Anthropologists, AIE for Folklorists, ARI for Curators) are near-exact reorderings
of IAR — confirming Holland Code compatibility at the structural level.

## Evidence

- **Investigative (I)**: Research methodology, systematic comparison, source
  verification — the core activity of anthropology, folklore, and religious studies
- **Artistic (A)**: Creative interpretation of symbolic systems, narrative
  construction, meaning-making — the interpretive dimension of cultural research
- **Realistic (R)**: Fieldwork, material culture, hands-on artifact work, practical
  methodology — the tangible dimension of anthropology and museum curation

No E (Enterprising) or S (Social) as primary codes — these are contraindicated for
Dan's profile (Persuasive 32%, Social Service 21%).

## Confidence: MEDIUM

Framework-inference only. Direct Holland Code assessment has not been administered.
Based on Birkman-to-Holland inference methodology.

## Implication

This claim establishes that the Holland Code framework — the most empirically
validated career matching instrument — supports cultural/anthropological career
paths. It does not argue FOR pursuing these paths; it documents the structural
compatibility.
