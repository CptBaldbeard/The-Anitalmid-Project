# anitalmid-birkman-16: Low Social Service interest contraindicates helping-profession IT roles

**Source:** [[Dan Bechtel Birkman Profile]] — Birkman Basics Report (G6HVDQ, 2025-09-05)

## The Claim

Dan's Social Service interest at 21% (ranking 8th of 10 interest areas) is a strong negative signal for helping-profession roles in IT: pure helpdesk, service desk management, customer success, IT training/education, and end-user support management. Social Service measures intrinsic motivation to help, serve, and care for others — the core motivational driver for support careers. At 21%, Dan does not derive satisfaction from helping as an end in itself.

## Evidence

- Social Service: 21% (8th of 10)
- Gap analysis: 76 points below Musical (97%) — among the largest differentials in the profile
- Gap analysis: 61 points below Technical (82%) — strong preference for systems over service
- Career trajectory: AppleCare (2015) → ops/security (2022-Present) — consistent move away from support

## Confidence

**Rating:** very-high

**Rationale:** Quantitative Birkman score. Career trajectory corroborates over 10+ years.

## Stakes

This is a companion claim to low Administrative and low Persuasive interests. Together, these three signals create a clear profile: Dan is drawn to systems and analysis, not to people-service, persuasion, or process administration.

## Edges

**Supports:**
- [[claim-pure-helpdesk-contraindicated]] — Social Service is the primary Birkman contraindication for helpdesk
- [[claim-persuasive-interest-low]] — Social Service + Persuasive together strongly gate against people-facing IT roles

## Assessment

21% Social Service is unambiguous. Combined with the career trajectory away from support, this is one of the clearest negative signals in the profile.
