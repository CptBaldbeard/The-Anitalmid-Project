# anitalmid-enneagram-1: Dan maps to Enneagram Type 5w6 (Investigator/Loyalist)

**Source:** [[Frameworks/Birkman-to-Framework Crosswalk]] — Section: Crosswalk 2 — Enneagram

## The Claim

Dan Bechtel's Birkman profile maps to Enneagram **Type 5 (Investigator)** with a
**Type 6 wing (Loyalist)** and a **Self-Preservation (SP) instinctual variant**. This
combination describes someone driven by the need for competence and knowledge (Type 5),
grounded by security-conscious systematic thinking (Wing 6), and oriented toward
creating stable, well-resourced environments (SP instinct).

## Evidence

### Type 5 (Investigator) evidence:
- Scientific interest 92% — Type 5's core domain
- Investigating strength (#1 of 11) — the primary Type 5 behavior
- Blue Interests: abstract thinking, future-orientation, theory
- Ambiguity handling strength — Type 5s thrive in complexity
- Reflective efficiency — Type 5s conserve energy, value deep focus
- Blue Usual Behavior: insightful, thoughtful, reflective — classic Type 5 descriptors

### Wing 6 (Loyalist) evidence:
- Yellow Needs: security, consistency, clear rules — Type 6 motivation
- Structured thinking strength — Type 6 systematic approach
- Stress pattern: rigid, rule-bound under pressure — unhealthy Type 6
- Security operations experience (career-1) — Type 6 behavioral evidence
- Collaborative "everyone wins" strength — Type 6 loyalty to team

### Self-Preservation (SP) instinct evidence:
- Yellow Needs for organized, systematic environment
- Prefers time alone or small groups (1-2 others)
- Reflective efficiency: conserves energy
- Low Persuasive interest (32%): avoids competitive visibility
- Stress pattern: withdrawal, not confrontation

## Confidence

**Rating:** medium
**Rationale:** Inferred from Birkman data rather than direct Enneagram assessment. Type 5 core type has strong Birkman alignment (Scientific + Investigating + Blue abstraction). Wing 6 and SP instinct are well-supported but involve more inference. The Enneagram framework has the weakest empirical validation of all five frameworks, which also limits confidence.

## Stakes

Type 5 is the archetypal "knowledge worker" — someone who needs to master complex
domains and contribute deep expertise. The 5w6 subtype in particular describes
someone who builds secure, well-engineered systems — exactly the IT architecture
and security roles the Capstone recommends. If Dan were a Type 4 (Individualist)
or Type 9 (Peacemaker), the career recommendations would shift significantly
toward creative or consensus-building roles.

The motivational dimension is the Enneagram's unique contribution: it predicts
not just career APTITUDE (will they be good at it?) but career SATISFACTION
(will they find it meaningful?). Type 5s are satisfied by deep expertise and
intellectual autonomy. Roles that offer surface-level variety without depth
will under-serve this motivation regardless of aptitude.

## Assessment

The Enneagram inference adds motivational depth that Birkman alone cannot provide.
Birkman tells us Dan is a Blue/Yellow bridge — Enneagram tells us WHY: he is driven
by the need for competence and knowledge (Type 5), made secure by structured systems
(Wing 6). This motivational framework should inform the Action Plan: certifications
and role progressions should be framed as KNOWLEDGE MASTERY (Type 5 satisfaction)
rather than CAREER ADVANCEMENT (Type 3 satisfaction).
