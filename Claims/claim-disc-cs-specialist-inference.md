# anitalmid-disc-1: Dan maps to High-C / Medium-S DISC (CS Specialist)

**Source:** [[Frameworks/Birkman-to-Framework Crosswalk]] — Section: Crosswalk 3 — DISC

## The Claim

Dan Bechtel's Birkman profile maps to a DISC behavioral style with **Very High C
(Conscientiousness)** and **Medium-High S (Steadiness)** — the CS "Specialist" or
"Perfectionist" blend — with Low D (Dominance) and Low I (Influence). This describes
someone who is systematic, precise, patient, methodical, and fact-oriented; who
prefers working with systems and data over people and persuasion.

## Evidence

### C — Conscientiousness (Very High)
- Structured thinking strength (#7 of 11) — core C behavior
- Yellow Needs: organized, systematic, consistent — the C environment preference
- Scientific interest 92% — drives analytical, precise work
- Stress pattern: over-insistent on rules — exaggerated C under pressure
- Technical interest 82% — precision-oriented domain
- Investigating strength — methodical, thorough approach

### S — Steadiness (Medium-High)
- One-on-one sensitivity strength (#6) — S's interpersonal style
- Emotional intelligence strength (#10) — S's empathy dimension
- Yellow Needs: consistent environment, trust — S's stability need
- "Everyone wins" collaborative strength (#8) — S's team orientation
- Stress: quietly resistive, reluctant to confront — S stress pattern

### D — Dominance (Low)
- Low Persuasive interest (32%) — D's primary domain
- Stress: reluctant to confront — explicitly contraindicative of high D
- Blue Usual Behavior: reflective, not assertive

### I — Influence (Low)
- Selectively sociable (Blue Usual Behavior characteristic)
- Low Persuasive interest (32%) — I's primary domain
- Introverted Usual Behavior pattern

## Confidence

**Rating:** medium
**Rationale:** Inferred from Birkman data, not from direct DISC assessment. The C dimension has very high confidence from multiple converging Birkman signals. S is well-supported. D and I lows are clear from both interest scores and behavioral descriptions. The CS blend label is the strongest inference.

## Stakes

DISC provides the BEHAVIORAL complement to Birkman's internal-drive model. It answers:
"How will Dan actually behave in a workplace?" The CS profile predicts:
- He will be systematic and precise (C), patient and supportive (S)
- He will NOT be assertive or competitive (Low D) or socially dominant (Low I)
- He communicates formally and factually, not emotionally or persuasively
- He needs clear expectations and time to produce quality work (S + C)

This has direct implications for ROLE ENVIRONMENT fit. A CS person will thrive in
structured, stable environments with clear standards (IT operations, compliance)
and struggle in fast-paced, high-social, constantly-changing environments (startups,
sales-driven organizations, highly political workplaces).

## Assessment

The CS DISC inference adds behavioral specificity to the career recommendations.
It explains not just WHICH roles fit but HOW Dan will perform in them — methodically,
precisely, supportively. It also predicts WHERE he'll struggle: high-D environments
(cutthroat competition), high-I environments (constant social stimulation), and
roles requiring aggressive persuasion or rapid improvisation without structure.
