# anitalmid-birkman-14: Blue Interests drive toward architecture and strategy roles

**Source:** [[Dan Bechtel Birkman Profile]] — Birkman Basics Report (G6HVDQ, 2025-09-05)

## The Claim

Dan's Interests ✱ profile falls entirely in the BLUE quadrant, with seven descriptors that form a coherent motivational pattern: "innovate or create," "plan how to do things," "consider the future," "create new approaches," "look at things theoretically," "deal with abstraction," "think of new approaches," "work with ideas." This is a future-oriented, creation-focused motivational engine. He is intrinsically drawn to designing new systems, planning architectures, and solving novel problems — not to maintaining existing systems, executing established procedures, or performing routine operational tasks.

## Evidence

### Birkman Interests ✱ (BLUE quadrant, all 7 descriptors):
- "innovate or create"
- "plan how to do things"
- "consider the future"
- "create new approaches"
- "look at things theoretically"
- "deal with abstraction"
- "think of new approaches"
- "work with ideas"

### Corroborating signals:
- Usual Behavior ◆ also in BLUE: "reflective and creative"
- Strength #5: "respond well to sound... interested in music, the spoken word, or any media which have an audio component" — creative orientation extends beyond IT
- Career Area #3: Computer & Mathematical Science — the channel for Blue creativity in IT

### What is notably absent:
- No Red (Doer) interest descriptors: no "compete," "take action quickly," "work with your hands"
- No Green (Communicator) interest descriptors: no "persuade," "teach," "sell"

## Confidence

**Rating:** very-high

**Rationale:** Seven descriptors all pointing in the same direction. Corroborated by Usual Behavior quadrant and creative strengths. The ABSENCE of competing interest descriptors is as informative as their presence.

## Stakes

This claim distinguishes design/build roles from operate/maintain roles. It suggests Dan's career satisfaction depends on having a CREATIVE component — not just executing established playbooks, but designing new ones. This pushes recommendations toward architecture, engineering, and strategy roles and away from pure operations.

## Assessment

Blue Interests are unambiguous and strong. Every descriptor points toward creation, planning, and ideas. The practical implication: even in an operations role, Dan needs a design/improvement component (automation, architecture evolution, process redesign) to stay engaged. Pure maintenance roles will drain him regardless of technical fit.
