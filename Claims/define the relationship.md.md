# Relationship Definitions
- **Supports**: Indicates that the linked claim provides evidence for this claim.
- [[supported_claim_1.md]]
- **Contradicts**: Indicates that the linked claim cannot coexist with this one.
- [[contradicting_claim_1.md]]
- **Depends On**: Indicates that this claim's validity is contingent upon the linked claim being true.
- [[dependent_claim_1.md]]
- **Relates**: Indicates that the linked claim is directly related to another claim.
- [[related_claims_1.md]]
