# anitalmid-birkman-7: Low Persuasive interest suggests avoiding sales-adjacent IT roles

**Source:** [[Dan Bechtel Birkman Profile]] — Birkman Basics Report (G6HVDQ, 2025-09-05)

## The Claim

Dan's Persuasive interest at 32% (7th of 10) signals low intrinsic attraction to selling, persuading, negotiating, or influencing others' decisions. This suggests avoiding: sales engineering, solutions consulting, pre-sales technical roles, vendor relationship management, and IT procurement negotiation. Roles where technical depth is the primary requirement and occasional persuasion is incidental (e.g., presenting architecture decisions to stakeholders) remain viable.

## Evidence

- Persuasive interest: 32% (7th of 10)
- Social Service interest: 21% (reinforcing pattern — low people-influence interests)
- Stress Behavior ■: "reluctant to confront others" — persuasion-required roles could trigger stress
- Strength #6: "sensitive and insightful in one-on-one relationships" — note: this is empathy, not persuasion
- Career history: AppleCare chat advisor involved customer interaction but as problem-solving, not selling

## Confidence

**Rating:** high

**Rationale:** Quantitative score + corroborating low Social Service + stress behavior pattern. However, 32% is not extremely low — the claim gates sales-heavy roles but not consultative technical roles.

## Stakes

This gates sales engineering from the recommendation set. Sales engineering is a common career suggestion for technically-skilled people with communication ability — but Dan's communication strength is Literary (writing) not Persuasive (speaking/selling).

## Edges

**Supports:**
- [[claim-systems-administrator-primary-fit]] — Systems administration is builder-focused, not sales-focused
- [[claim-literary-interest-92-technical-writing]] — Literary (writing) is the communication channel, not Persuasive (speaking/selling)

**Contradicts:**
- [[claim-one-on-one-sensitivity-strength]] — Apparent contradiction: sensitivity to others could be confused with persuasiveness. The Birkman data distinguishes them clearly — Dan understands people (sensitivity) but doesn't want to influence them (low Persuasive).

## Assessment

32% Persuasive interest is a moderate negative signal. It firmly gates pure sales engineering but leaves technical advisory roles open. The distinction between Literary communication (writing, documentation) and Persuasive communication (selling, negotiating) is one of the profile's most actionable insights for career direction.
