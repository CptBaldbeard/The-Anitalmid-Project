## The Claim

Dan Bechtel's Enneagram type, as measured by a direct Enneagram assessment, is
**Type 4 (The Individualist)**. The previously inferred Type 5w6 was incorrect
as a core type but correctly identified the analytical/systematic dimension as
a strong secondary influence. The corrected type is **4w5** (Individualist with
Investigator wing) — the Type 4 core drive for authenticity, creative
self-expression, and meaning, combined with a Type 5 wing that supplies
intellectual depth, analytical rigor, and systematic thinking.

### Why 4w5 Is the Correct Fit

The 4w5 archetype describes someone who:
- Is driven by creative authenticity and meaningful self-expression (Type 4 core)
- Approaches this with intellectual depth and analytical rigor (Type 5 wing)
- Expresses creativity through structured, well-researched work (4w5 blend)
- May appear more reserved and analytical than typical Type 4s (Wing 5 influence)
- Combines emotional depth with intellectual curiosity

This resolves the apparent tension between Dan's high Artistic/Musical/Literary
interests (Type 4 domain) and his high Scientific/Technical interests (Type 5 domain).
They are not competing drives — they are a core type and its supporting wing.
