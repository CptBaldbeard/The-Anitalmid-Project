# anitalmid-career-10: GRC SaaS platform documentation at Valdyr.io

**Source:** [[Resume/Dan Bechtel Resume]] — Valdyr.io, 2024-2025

## The Claim

As a freelance Technical Writer for Valdyr.io, Dan developed security and compliance documentation for a GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance) SaaS platform. This work involved translating ISO 27001, SOC2, and data privacy framework requirements into documentation accessible to both engineering and business audiences. Unlike his internal compliance work at City of Moscow (CJIS), this role was product-facing — he documented security controls and risk management procedures for a GRC software product, not just for an internal IT department.

  - "Collaborated with engineering and business teams to document security controls and risk management procedures"
- Duration: 2024-2025 (freelance, concurrent with City of Moscow role)
- Frameworks covered: ISO 27001, SOC2, data privacy

## Stakes

This claim differentiates Dan's compliance experience from purely internal IT compliance. Most IT professionals who document compliance do so for their own organization's audits. Dan has done it for a GRC SaaS PRODUCT — meaning he understands compliance from the vendor side, not just the consumer side. This is a career differentiator for GRC Analyst and compliance-focused security roles.
