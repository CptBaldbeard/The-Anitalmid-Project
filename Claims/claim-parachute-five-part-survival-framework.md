# anitalmid-parachute-4: Bolles structures job-hunting as a five-part survival skill

**Source:** [[Parachute/What Color Is Your Parachute Framework]] — 2012 edition, Bolles (1970, revised annually)

## The Claim

Bolles organizes What Color Is Your Parachute? around a five-part survival framework that structurally parallels the Anitalmid OSKG pipeline. The five parts are: (I) Attitudes Necessary for Survival, (II) Advanced Job-Finding Techniques, (III) Advanced Job-Creation Techniques, (IV) Inventory of What You Have to Offer the World, and (V) Each One Teach One. This framework recognizes that job-hunting during economic contraction requires a fundamentally different approach than job-hunting during expansion — it is a survival skill, not merely a search.

## Evidence

### The Five-Part Architecture

| Part | Focus | Key Chapters | OSKG Parallel |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I. Attitudes** | Emotional/psychological foundation | Ch.3 (Three Attitudes), Ch.4 (Handicaps) | Confidence ratings, Birkman Needs/Stress awareness |
| **II. Job-Finding Techniques** | Finding existing vacancies | Ch.5 (Best/Worst Ways), Ch.6 (Resume), Ch.7 (Networking), Ch.8 (Interviewing), Ch.9 (Salary) | Career History claims (evidence of competency) |
| **III. Job-Creation Techniques** | Creating work that doesn't exist yet | Ch.10 (Starting a Business), Ch.11 (Inventiveness), Ch.12 (Choosing a New Career) | Role Profile Expansion, Counter-Position Tests |
| **IV. Self-Inventory** | Understanding what you offer | Ch.13 (The Flower Exercise: WHAT skills, WHERE you want to use them, HOW to find job names) | Claims extraction, Birkman-to-Framework Crosswalk |
| **V. Each One Teach One** | Teaching others as reinforcement | Ch.14 (Teaching Survival Job-Hunting) + Pink Pages (Appendices A-D) | Session log, documentation, public GitHub repo |

### The Survival Analogy

Bolles uses an extended metaphor throughout the book: job-hunting skills are like a life jacket — you don't need them when the water is calm (good economy), but when you capsize (recession, layoff), they determine whether you drown. The five-part framework IS the life jacket. This metaphor appears in Chapter 2 and recurs throughout.

### What Makes This Claim Significant

1. **Structural parallel to OSKG**: The Anitalmid project unconsciously replicated Bolles' architecture — Self-Inventory (claims extraction) → Techniques (role profiles, structural analysis) → Job-Creation (career recommendations) → Teaching Others (documentation, GitHub). Bolles' explicit framework validates the project's implicit methodology.

2. **Attitudes as FOUNDATION**: Bolles places attitudes (Ch.3-4) BEFORE techniques (Ch.5-9). This is counterintuitive for a "job-hunting manual" but reflects his 40+ years of counseling experience: emotional state predicts job-search outcomes more reliably than technique quality. This maps to the Birkman stress/needs awareness layer of the project.

3. **Job-Creation as distinct from Job-Finding**: Bolles devotes an entire section (3 chapters) to creating work that doesn't exist yet, separate from finding existing vacancies. This validates the project's "progression path" modeling (Cloud Admin doesn't exist in Dan's current org but can be created through certification + experience).

## Stakes

This framework matters because it validates the Anitalmid project's architecture as methodologically sound. The project's pipeline (claims → structural analysis → recommendations → documentation) independently arrived at a structure Bolles developed over 40 years of career counseling. This is not coincidence — it reflects both frameworks' shared insight that career development requires: (1) emotional foundation, (2) evidence-based competency analysis, (3) creative problem-solving, and (4) systematic methodology.

## Assessment

Bolles' five-part framework provides the missing "operating system" for the Anitalmid project. The project has the data layer (claims, edges, frameworks) and the analysis layer (structural analysis, convergence). Bolles provides the execution layer: a field-tested methodology for converting self-knowledge into career outcomes. The structural parallel between the five-part framework and the OSKG pipeline is strong enough to treat Bolles as a methodological validator — not just another data source.
