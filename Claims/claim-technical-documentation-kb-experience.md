This demonstrates two competencies: (1) the ability to analyze existing procedures and identify standardization opportunities, and (2) the ability to create durable technical documentation (KB articles) that other technicians can follow. The measurable outcome (reduced resolution time) proves the documentation was effective, not just complete.
