# anitalmid-birkman-3: Scientific interest at 92% supports analytical IT roles

**Source:** [[Dan Bechtel Birkman Profile]] — Birkman Basics Report (G6HVDQ, 2025-09-05)

## The Claim

Dan's Scientific interest at 92% signals a strong intrinsic attraction to investigation, systematic inquiry, and analytical problem-solving. This maps directly to IT roles with significant analytical components: security analysis (threat hunting, log analysis, forensics), systems architecture (root cause analysis, capacity planning), and compliance/GRC (control assessment, risk analysis).

## Evidence

- Scientific interest: 92% (tied with Literary for 3rd-highest)
- Career Area #2: Life, Physical, & Social Science — "applying scientific knowledge and expertise"
- Career Area #3: Computer & Mathematical Science — "designing, developing, and maintaining databases, software, hardware, networks"
- Strength #1: "investigating situations — often in a research or trouble-shooting capacity"
- Strength #11: "can handle ambiguous situations well, where the best answer may be less obvious"

## Confidence

**Rating:** very-high

**Rationale:** Quantitative score corroborated by two career area rankings and two strength statements.

## Stakes

Scientific interest distinguishes analytical IT roles (security analysis, architecture, forensics) from purely operational IT roles (helpdesk, break-fix, routine administration). Without this signal, the recommendation would skew toward operational rather than analytical career paths.

## Assessment

92% Scientific interest is unequivocally strong. The pairing with Literary at 92% (claim-literary-interest-92-technical-writing) is the profile's most distinctive pattern: Dan is drawn to analytical work AND to communicating findings clearly. This is rare and valuable in IT — most technical professionals are strong in one, not both.
