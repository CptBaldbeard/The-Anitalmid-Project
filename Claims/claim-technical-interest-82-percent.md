# anitalmid-birkman-2: Technical interest at 82% supports IT career alignment

**Source:** [[Dan Bechtel Birkman Profile]] — Birkman Basics Report (G6HVDQ, 2025-09-05)

## The Claim

Dan's Birkman Interests profile shows Technical interest at 82%, ranking 5th out of 10 interest areas. This is a strong positive signal for IT career alignment — Technical interest measures attraction to working with technology, tools, systems, and technical problem-solving. Combined with Scientific interest (92%, ranked 4th) and Literary interest (92%, ranked 3rd), the top-5 interest profile forms a coherent cluster around analytical, systems-oriented, and communication-heavy technical work.

## Evidence

### Interest rankings (top 5 of 10):
1. Musical: 97%
2. Artistic: 94%
3. Literary: 92%
4. Scientific: 92%
5. Technical: 82%

### Gap analysis:
- Technical (82%) is 42 points above Administrative (20%) — a 5x differential
- Technical (82%) is 50 points above Numerical (17%) — suggesting attraction to applied technology over pure computation
- Technical (82%) is 50 points above Social Service (21%) — suggesting preference for systems over direct people-service

### Career Area corroboration:
- Computer & Mathematical Science ranks #3 in Top Career Areas, directly validating Technical interest

## Confidence

**Rating:** very-high

**Rationale:** Quantitative Birkman score — no interpretation required. The ranking within the full interest profile provides robust context.

## Stakes

Technical interest is a threshold claim for IT career recommendations. If Technical interest were below 50%, the case for IT careers would depend entirely on transferable skills from other domains. At 82%, Technical interest is a positive driver — Dan is drawn TO technical work, not just capable of it.

82% Technical interest is a strong, unambiguous signal. The 42-point gap between Technical and Administrative is particularly informative — it distinguishes Dan's profile from someone who tolerates technology as part of a clerical workflow. Dan is drawn to the technology itself.
