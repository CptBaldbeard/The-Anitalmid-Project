# anitalmid-career-3: Compliance and security documentation experience across two roles

**Source:** [[Career History/Technical_Writer]] (Valdyr.io, 2024-2025) and [[Career History/Information_Systems_Specialist]] (City of Moscow, 2022-Present)

## The Claim

Dan has direct, documented experience writing compliance and security documentation in two different contexts: as a Technical Writer producing GRC SaaS platform documentation covering ISO 27001, SOC2, and data privacy frameworks, and as an Information Systems Specialist maintaining security documentation for CJIS Level 4 compliance. This dual experience — understanding compliance frameworks AND producing compliant documentation — is the core competency for GRC Analyst, Compliance Engineer, and Security Documentation Specialist roles.

**Rating:** very-high

This claim opens GRC and compliance engineering career paths that would be inaccessible without demonstrated writing ability.
