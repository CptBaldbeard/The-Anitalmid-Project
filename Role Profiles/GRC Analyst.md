# GRC Analyst — Role Profile

## Overview

The Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) Analyst ensures an organization's information security program meets regulatory, contractual, and industry-standard requirements. This role designs and tests security controls, manages the risk register, authors policies and procedures, and shepherds the organization through audits and certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS, CJIS, HIPAA). For Dan, this is a **differentiated specialization** that uniquely leverages his technical writing talent, compliance experience, and structured thinking — he has already done this work at Valdyr.io as a [[Career History/Technical_Writer|Technical Writer]].

## Why This Role Matters

Regulatory pressure on organizations has never been higher: GDPR fines exceeded €4 billion cumulatively by 2025, SEC cybersecurity disclosure rules now require public companies to report material incidents within four business days, and cyber insurance underwriters increasingly demand evidence of mature GRC programs before issuing policies. The GRC Analyst bridges the gap between technical security operations and business accountability — without this role, organizations achieve neither certification nor insurability. The ISACA State of Cybersecurity report consistently identifies GRC as one of the most understaffed and fastest-growing cybersecurity domains.
