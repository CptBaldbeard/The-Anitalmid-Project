# Independent Cultural Researcher / Writer

## Role Overview

The independent cultural researcher investigates historical and anthropological
questions outside the academy — writing books, producing content (YouTube, podcasts,
Substack/newsletters), consulting for media productions, and building a public
audience around cultural history, mythology, and belief systems.

This path captures the research INTEREST without the academic CREDENTIAL. It asks:
*What if you just started doing the research you want to do, right now, and found
an audience for it?*

Examples of this career model:
- **Joseph Campbell** — independent scholar of comparative mythology (The Hero's Journey)
- **Tom Holland** (the historian, not Spider-Man) — popular history writer without a history PhD
- **Dan Carlin** — "Hardcore History" podcast, amateur historian with massive audience
- **Lindybeige / Overly Sarcastic Productions** — YouTube creators making mythology/history accessible

## Why This Matches Dan's Profile

This is the LOWEST-BARRIER path to doing the work Dan described. It requires:

- **Research skills** (Scientific 92%, Investigating #1) — already possessed
- **Writing ability** (Literary 92%, Written word strength) — already demonstrated
- **Creative presentation** (Artistic 94%, Visual appeal, videographer experience) — already demonstrated
- **Self-direction** (INTJ autonomy, Birkman Need for quiet/independence) — the ideal work environment
- **Systematic thinking** (INTJ Ni-Te, Structured thinking) — producing coherent book/video series

The critical insight: Dan already has ALL the skills needed to research and write
about mythology, anthropology, and cultural belief systems. The only thing missing
is STARTING. No degree, no permission, no gatekeepers.

### Possible formats:
- **Non-fiction books** — trade press cultural history (e.g., "How Geography Shaped the Gods")
- **Substack/newsletter** — serialized deep-dives into specific cultures' belief systems
- **YouTube channel** — video essays on mythology/legends (leverages videographer experience)
- **Podcast** — interview-based or narrative cultural history
- **Historical consultant** — advising game studios, filmmakers on cultural/mythological accuracy
- **Blog/website** — long-form essays on comparative belief systems

### Gaps

- No published book or established content channel
- Income path is uncertain — "build audience, then monetize" model
- Requires entrepreneurial/self-promotion skills (Persuasive 32% is a tension)
- No formal credentials in the subject matter (can affect credibility perception)

### Entry Path
- **Start now**: Choose one platform, one topic, publish one piece. Repeat.
- **Side project**: Begin alongside current employment; transition if/when income materializes
- **Build to book**: Substack → audience → book proposal → traditional or self-publishing
- **Consulting**: Niche expertise → consulting for media (games, film, publishing)
