# Instructional Designer / Education Technologist

## Role Overview

Instructional designers create educational experiences and learning materials
using systematic design methodologies. They analyze learner needs, design
curricula, develop content (e-learning modules, instructor-led materials,
assessments), and evaluate learning outcomes. Education technologists add
a technology layer — LMS administration, authoring tools, multimedia production.

This role combines three of Dan's strongest Birkman areas: Literary (content
creation), Artistic (creative design), and Technical (learning technology).
