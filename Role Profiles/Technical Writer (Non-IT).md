# Technical Writer

## Role Overview

Technical writers create documentation that translates complex technical
information into clear, accessible content for specific audiences. They bridge
the gap between engineers/developers and end users, producing manuals, guides,
API documentation, knowledge bases, white papers, and online help systems.

## Why This Matches Dan's Profile

Dan has the single rarest combination for technical writing: technical depth
PLUS writing ability. Most technical writers come from English/communications
backgrounds and struggle with technical concepts, OR come from engineering
backgrounds and struggle with writing. Dan has BOTH:
