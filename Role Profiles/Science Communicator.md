# Science Communicator / Medical Writer

## Role Overview

Science communicators translate complex scientific and medical research into
accessible content for non-expert audiences. They work across media: articles,
press releases, educational materials, grant proposals, regulatory documents,
patient education, and public health communications. Medical writers specialize
in pharmaceutical/healthcare documentation (clinical study reports, regulatory
submissions, physician education).

## Why This Matches Dan's Profile

This role represents the convergence of Dan's THREE STRONGEST Birkman interests:

- **Scientific 92%** — the content domain (understanding complex research)
- **Literary 92%** — the communication medium (writing clearly)
- **Artistic 94%** — the creative presentation (visual, multimedia)

This is one of the very few careers where all three top interests operate
simultaneously at full strength. Combined with the investigating strength
(research), ambiguity handling (complex topics), and structured thinking
(organized presentation), this is arguably Dan's best NON-IT aptitude fit.
