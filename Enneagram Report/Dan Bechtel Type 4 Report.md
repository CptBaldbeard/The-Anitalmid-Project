### Why Birkman Mapped to Type 5

Birkman's Scientific interest (92%), Investigating strength, and Yellow Needs
(systematic environment) strongly suggested Type 5. These signals are REAL —
Dan IS analytical, systematic, and investigative. But Birkman measures WORK
BEHAVIOR, not core motivation.

The Enneagram measures CORE MOTIVATION — WHY someone does what they do. Dan's
drive is not primarily competence (Type 5) but AUTHENTICITY AND CREATIVE
SELF-EXPRESSION (Type 4). He USES analytical tools and systematic approaches
(manifesting as Type 5 behaviors) IN SERVICE OF creative, meaningful expression
(the Type 4 core drive).

### The 4w5 Resolution

The **4w5 (Individualist with Investigator wing)** perfectly resolves the tension:
- **Core Type 4**: Drive for authenticity, creative self-expression, meaning,
  emotional depth — aligns with Artistic 94%, Musical 97%, Literary 92%, Blue
  Interests (innovation, creating new approaches), written word, visual appeal
- **Wing 5**: Intellectual curiosity, analytical depth, systematic thinking,
  investigative approach — aligns with Scientific 92%, Technical 82%,
  Investigating strength, structured thinking, ambiguity handling

This is a MUCH better fit than the pure Type 5 inference. The Birkman Blue/Yellow
bridge IS the 4w5 cognitive architecture: Blue = Type 4 creative/emotional core;
Yellow = Type 5 wing systematic/analytical execution.
